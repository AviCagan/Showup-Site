Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This page says what ShowUp stores, where, who can see it, and how to remove it. It is written from the app's actual data flows, not from a template. If something here does not match what the app does, tell us and we will fix one or the other.

In short

  • Your workouts, food log, bodyweight and everything Ares works out from them are stored on your phone only.
  • You sign in once, with email or Google, and everything then works offline. The account is how you keep your username and add friends. Friends see counts and percentages you chose to share, never your logs.
  • No advertising, no analytics SDKs, no selling of data. Progress photos you take stay on your phone and are never analyzed; the app copies them to your own Google Drive only if you turn that on, and never sends them to us. A meal photo you choose to send is analyzed once and never stored. The app is for people 16 and older.
  • The app counts which of its screens and features are used and for how long, to learn what helps. The counts carry a random number made on your phone, never your account, and never anything you log or type. While the app is in testing there is no switch to turn this off; Delete all data gives your phone a new random number (section 6, Usage statistics).
  • Google Drive backup, once you connect it, copies the parts you switch on from your phone straight to a folder in your own Google Drive, by itself (your data unless you switch it off, progress photos only if you switch that on); it never passes through our server. "Save backup file" makes one file of everything and hands it to your phone's share sheet, so it goes only where you pick.
  • When you chat with Ares, what is on the screen you asked from (for example your logged sets and loads, your targets, the day's food entries, or during a workout the injury areas you marked) and, from any screen, a summary of your own data (your plan, the last 4 weeks of training, your weekly report, nutrition targets and averages, weight trend and goal, injuries and today; the full list is in section 6 (g)) go to the language model through our server for that answer only. They are never stored, and never sent with your name, email or account id.
  • When you ask Ares to change something (log a set or a food, change a setting), the change happens on your phone with the app's own numbers, and what it did goes back to the language model for the rest of that answer. A setting kept with your account (your notifications and What friends see) is also saved there, just as when you change it on its screen. Notes you ask Ares to remember stay on your phone; they go with each chat question and with the requests that word Ares's findings, the weekly report, a plan's pros and cons and a Plan review (minus notes on topics you switched off), and shape only the wording.
  • Your location stays on your phone. A gym's saved location is used only by the phone to notice you arrive on a day you train with a friend, and only if you switch that on; your friend is told "arrived", never where. What you type into a gym's address goes through our server to an OpenStreetMap search (Photon, run by komoot) for suggestions, held only briefly in our server's memory (section 6).
  • If you connect Health Connect on Android, ShowUp reads your steps, distance, other apps' workouts and weigh-ins onto your phone and saves your ShowUp workouts to Health Connect. Only weekly activity summaries reach Ares's language model, through our server, and weigh-ins from Health Connect join the weight trend Ares already sends. Health data is never sold or used for ads (details).

2. What stays on your phone

The app keeps its data in a database inside its own private storage on your phone. Nothing in this list is sent to us or to anyone else:

  • Workouts: exercises, sets, weights, reps, RIR, set types, notes, duration, templates.
  • Your food log: entries, calories, macros, custom foods, recipes, daily totals.
  • Your bodyweight log.
  • Profile inputs: training level, sex at birth (which only selects the body model), birth year and month, height, goal, calorie and macro targets, equipment, units.
  • Everything the app computes from those: muscle coverage, your weekly targets, the weekly report, personal records, Ares's findings, trajectory and check-in, streaks, and each two-weekly Plan review with the changes it suggested, what you applied or undid, and the day it was due.
  • Rest-timer, reminder and nudge settings. Those notifications are scheduled on the phone.
  • What Ares remembers: the short notes you asked Ares to remember in the chat (at most 40, in your words), listed under Settings, Ares, What Ares remembers, where you can forget each one. They are left out of the JSON and Google Drive backups, and Delete all data clears them. They are the one item here that leaves the phone: each chat question and the narration requests in section 6 ((a), (f), (g) and (j)) carry them through our server to the language model for that answer only, and they are never stored there.
  • Progress photos (front, side and back) you take after a workout, after a weigh-in or from the Photos pane. They are JPEG files in the app's private folder, not in your gallery. The app never sends them to our server or to any model, and never looks at what is in them; the one upload is to your own Google Drive, and only if you switch on "Progress photos" in the Google Drive backup's "What to back up" (below). They are left out of the JSON backup; "Export photos" under Data and backup makes a separate zip that you hand to your phone's share sheet yourself, and "Import photos" reads that zip back. The zip is written to the app's cache, one at a time: the next export, deleting a photo, and Delete all data remove it.
  • Your gyms: a name, an optional address, and, only if you tap "Use my current location", the gym's coordinates. The coordinates are used for one thing: with "Tell my buddy when I arrive" switched on (it is off until you turn it on, in Settings > Social > Gyms), on a day you are in a gym session with a friend, the phone asks its own location service to notice when you enter a circle about 150 m around that gym. That check runs on the phone; the app never reads your position in the background and never sends it. The watch stops when you arrive, tap "I'm here", turn the switch off or delete your data. Once the session or the day is over it stops the next time you open the app or pass the gym; until then the circle stays registered with your phone's location service, but nothing is read or sent. Your gyms are in the JSON backup with their coordinates, because that file is yours; the app never uploads it. Typing in a gym's address field (from 3 characters, after a short pause) sends the words to our server's address search, which asks Photon, an OpenStreetMap search run by komoot, and shows up to 5 places (section 6); picking one saves its address and point on the phone, the same as "Use my current location".

You can export all of it as CSV or JSON from the app (photos through their own zip) and delete all of it in Settings under Data and backup; Delete all data removes the photo files and any photo or backup zip too. Uninstalling the app deletes it too.

Google Drive backup. Off until you connect it under Settings, Data and backup (Android). "Back up to Google Drive" first opens "What to back up", with one switch for each part the backup can copy: "Workouts, food and settings" (the JSON backup), on until you turn it off, and "Progress photos", off until you turn it on. The switches keep your choice, and the automatic backup copies the same parts. Connecting shows Google's own consent screen and asks for one permission: to see, create and change only the files ShowUp itself creates in your Drive (Google calls it the drive.file scope); the app cannot see anything else in your Drive. From then on the phone backs up by itself when you open the app (at most about twice a day), after you finish a workout and after you save progress photos, and when you tap "Back up" in "What to back up": it copies the parts switched on into a folder named "ShowUp backup" in your Google Drive. With "Workouts, food and settings" on, that is the JSON backup (everything in the list above, your gyms included) as backup.json, keeping the one before it as backup-previous.json. With "Progress photos" on, each photo is copied once, under its file name, into a "photos" folder there, with photos.json (each photo's date, kind, angle, caption, size and your bodyweight at the time). A photo you delete on the phone is deleted from that folder at the next backup; nothing else there is ever deleted by the app, and turning a switch off stops new copies of that part and leaves what is already there. The upload goes from your phone straight to Google: it never passes through our server, we keep no copy, and we never see your Google account or its password. The phone asks Google Play services for a short-lived access token each time and keeps no Google password or long-lived token. The files count against your Google storage, and Google's privacy policy covers them. "Disconnect Google Drive" removes the app's access to your Drive when the phone is online; if it is not, the app says so, and you can remove the access in your Google Account. The folder stays in your Drive until you delete it, and so it does after Delete all data. "Restore from Drive" reads the backup back through the same Restore screen, then the photos this phone does not have. ShowUp's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements: the Drive access is used only to write and read your own backup for you, on your phone, never for advertising, and no person at ShowUp reads it.

Save backup file. Under Settings, Data and backup, "Save backup file" writes one zip, showup-backup-<date>.zip, holding the JSON backup and your progress photos, to the app's cache and opens your phone's share sheet. You choose where it goes: Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, Files, or any other app on your phone, or nowhere. The app never uploads it, has no account with any of those services, and never learns where you sent it; the receiving app's own terms cover the copy it keeps. Only one such zip sits in the cache at a time; the next backup or photo export, deleting a photo, and Delete all data remove it. "Restore a backup" reads the zip back: the rows first, then the photos, skipping photos already on the phone. An optional weekly reminder ("Remind me to back up", off until you turn it on) is a notification scheduled on the phone; while the Google Drive backup is on, copies your data and has run in the last week, it stays quiet.

Your phone's own backup. Your phone's automatic backup (Google backup on Android, iCloud Backup on iPhone) may include the app's database according to your device settings; that is between you and your phone's vendor, and the app never sends it anywhere itself. On Android the app tells that backup, and a transfer to a new phone, to leave the progress-photo folder out, so the photos never leave the phone that way and cannot push the app past Android's size limit for one app's backup. iPhone's iCloud Backup may include the progress photos; the app cannot exclude them there yet.

Health Connect (Android)

Health Connect is Android's on-device store for health and fitness data, shared by the apps you allow (watches, scales, running and cycling apps). ShowUp uses it only after you tap Connect in Settings, Data and backup, Health Connect, and only for the kinds of data you allow in Health Connect's own permission screen. Apple Health on iPhone is not connected in this version.

  • What ShowUp reads: your steps and distance per day; the exercise sessions other apps saved (the type of activity, start and end time, the title the other app gave it, and which app saved it); and your weigh-ins. It reads the last 35 days and keeps reading while you stay connected; Health Connect lets a new app see at most 30 days before you first allowed it.
  • What ShowUp writes: each workout you finish in ShowUp, as one exercise session with its type (strength training), start and end time and name, so other apps can see you trained. Editing or deleting the workout in ShowUp updates or deletes that session. Sets, weights and reps are not written.
  • Where it is kept: on your phone, in the app's database. Weigh-ins join your weight log (Nutrition, Weight) and feed Ares like any weigh-in, including the weight trend that reaches Anthropic (next point). Steps and other apps' sessions are a copy used for Ares's activity facts and a short list on the Health Connect screen; they are never shown as ShowUp workouts and never count toward muscle coverage. Because Health Connect already keeps them, what ShowUp read from it is left out of Export, of the JSON backup (so of the Google Drive backup too) and of the "Save backup file" zip, and Delete all data removes it. A weigh-in from Health Connect that you delete in ShowUp stays deleted.
  • What leaves your phone: when Ares writes its read or you ask Ares in the chat, weekly summaries of the last 7 full days go through our server to Anthropic (section 6) for that answer only: your average steps a day, the average over the 4 weeks before and the change between them, the total distance, the number of sessions and minutes other apps logged by type of activity, their average minutes a week over the 4 weeks before, and the active minutes of those sessions and your ShowUp workouts together. Never a single day's steps, single records, the titles or apps of other sessions, locations or heart rate (ShowUp does not read them), and nothing is stored on our server. You can turn this off with Activity in Settings, Ares, Not interested. Weigh-ins read from Health Connect are part of your weight log, so the latest one can be the current weight, and part of the weekly rate, in the Ares findings sent when Ares writes its read (section 6 (a)) and in the weight part of the summary each Ares chat question carries (section 6 (g)); muting Activity does not stop that, turning Read weight off stops new ones.
  • What we never do with it: sell it, give it to advertisers or data brokers, use it for advertising, or use it for credit, insurance or employment decisions.
  • Your controls: switch Read activity, Read weight and Save workouts on or off one by one; turning Read activity off deletes the copied steps and sessions. Disconnect stops all reading and writing, deletes the copied activity and asks Health Connect to remove ShowUp's access (Health Connect finishes it when ShowUp restarts). Weigh-ins already in your log stay until you delete them. Workouts ShowUp saved stay in Health Connect until you delete them there: Health Connect, App permissions, ShowUp, Delete data. Health Connect's permission screen links to this policy.

4. What is never stored on our server

These have no place on our server. There is no column for them, so no switch can turn them on and no bug in a setting can leak them. When you chat with Ares, the ones on your screen and in the summary of your own data (section 6 (g)) pass through our server to the language model for that one answer, and are not kept; your height, sex at birth and birth date never do:

  • Calories, macros, food entries and daily totals.
  • Bodyweight, height, sex at birth, birth date.
  • RIR, notes, and the weight or reps of any set. During a gym session they are relayed live to your partner and never written anywhere (section 5).
  • Weekly effective sets per muscle (friends see a status word, never the count).
  • Ares's findings, the check-in, the weekly report text, your Ares chats, and the notes Ares remembers.
  • Body or progress photos. Progress photos are kept on your phone, and in your own Google Drive only if you turn that on (section 2); no function, model or table on our side receives them. A meal photo you choose to send for food identification is analyzed once and never stored (section 6).
  • Your location, and your gyms' coordinates or addresses (section 2). Only a gym's name and the fact that you arrived are kept on our server. What you type into a gym's address passes through it to the address search and is held only in its memory, for at most 10 minutes, never in a table, a file or a log (section 6).
  • Anything read from Health Connect: steps, distance, other apps' sessions and weigh-ins (section 2). Weekly summaries of your activity pass through to the language model when Ares writes its read or you chat, and are not kept.

5. Who can see what

  • Nobody sees anything about you until you add a friend and they accept. Friends are found by exact username, a link you share, or a QR code on your screen.
  • Friends see your weekly card (per your switches), today's check if on, the workouts you chose to share, and what you post on theirs. Buddies see four weeks instead of one.
  • People in a challenge, event or chat with you see your username, display name and the values that feature needs.
  • In a gym session, the other person sees the gym's name and, once you arrive, "Sam is at the gym" (a push, if they allow it, and a line in the app). They never see where you are.
  • During a gym session you joined, your partner sees live: the exercise you are on, your set count, the weight and reps of each set you complete, your rest timer, and that your phone is still connected. Switch "Share weights with partner" off (in the session or in Settings > Social) and they see only the exercise and set count. This is relayed through our server as it happens and is never stored; when you leave, it is gone. Your warm-ups, RIR, notes and the workout's name are never sent.
  • Blocking someone removes the friendship and hides each of you from the other everywhere. They are not told.
  • There is no public profile, no search by name, no contact upload and no browsing. The database enforces these rules on every read, not only the app.
  • We, the people who run ShowUp, can read server data when needed for support, moderation and abuse prevention.

7. How long data is kept

  • On your phone: until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Messages: 30 days, then deleted by a nightly job.
  • Invite links and QR codes: they work for 7 days and are removed within 7 days after they expire. You can revoke all of yours at any time.
  • Daily target checks: 7 days.
  • Gym sessions: an open session ends 12 hours after its last activity (an invite, a join or an arrival); ended sessions are deleted after 30 days. The live sets were never stored.

10. Security

Data travels over HTTPS. Every server table enforces row-level security, so the database itself refuses reads outside your friendships and memberships, not only the app. Keys with wider access exist only on the server. Passwords are hashed by Supabase Auth; we never see them. Stored data is encrypted at rest by the hosting provider. No system is perfect; if you find a problem, write to us first so we can fix it.
