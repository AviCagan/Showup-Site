02 Logging

Log a set in two taps.

Every row shows what you did last time. Repeat it with one tap, or beat it.

  • A target for each set from Ares, updated after every set you finish
  • A rest timer on your lock screen, with Skip and +30 s
  • Supersets, drop sets, warm-ups, RIR or to failure, a plate calculator
  • Cardio with time and distance
  • Bring your history from Strong, Hevy and other apps

Ares Last time you did 60 kg × 8. Go for 62.5 kg × 8 this time.

03 Muscle map

See what your week covers.

32 regions on a 3D body you can turn. Each one goes from red to green as your weekly hard sets reach its target.

  • Targets from your own plan, or from published weekly-volume ranges
  • Female and male bodies, each in two styles
  • Tap a muscle for its sets and target this week
  • Colors mean coverage, never muscle size
  • No sets yet
  • Halfway
  • Target met

Ares Calves have no sets yet this week. Want me to add some to Friday?

04 Weekly report

What to add, move or cut.

Each week ShowUp checks every muscle group against its target and lists the fixes that matter most.

  • Plan my week: sessions checked against every group's target before you see them
  • The plan's pros and cons in plain words; swap, move or rebuild a day
  • Tell it about an injury and it leaves out what loads that area
  • New bests and lift trends in the same report

Ares Rear delts are short this week. Three sets of face pulls on Thursday would do it.

05 Ares

A coach that reads your numbers.

Ask Ares from almost any screen. It can change a setting, edit your workout or log a meal, and every change comes with Undo.

  • Anything that deletes or shares waits for your tap
  • Remembers what you tell it; see or clear it in Settings
  • Never makes up numbers: the app computes, Ares explains
  • Not interested in a topic? Say so, and it stops bringing it up

06 Nutrition

Log food without the busywork.

Search the catalog and online foods together, scan a barcode, or describe a meal in a sentence or a photo.

  • Ares names the foods and amounts; the app computes every number
  • A meal photo is analyzed once and never stored
  • Recipes, repeat meals, cal and macro rings, vitamins and minerals
  • Weigh-ins and a weight trend

Ares I found 2 eggs and 1 slice of toast. Check the amounts, then log.

07 Exercises

2,298 exercises that move.

Nearly every exercise in the 2,386-exercise catalog plays a short animation, with the muscles it trains marked in red.

  • Drag the figure to turn it
  • Technique videos and Ask Ares in every exercise sheet
  • An A to Z library with popular lifts first

Ares Not sure about the form? Ask me about any exercise.

08 Friends

Friends who see you show up.

Friends see your sessions this week, your coverage and your streak. They see weights only in a workout you share or a session you train together, and never your calorie numbers or bodyweight.

  • Accountability buddies and private groups
  • Challenges on sessions, coverage or days on target, never on weight lifted
  • Plan a gym session together and see when your partner arrives
  • Messages with reactions

Ares Want your training partner to see your sets but not your weights? I can switch that off.

09 Your data

Your logs live on your phone.

ShowUp works offline. Workouts, food and weight are stored on your phone, and you can take them with you.

  • Automatic backups to your own Google Drive
  • CSV and JSON export, and restore from a backup
  • Health Connect: steps and weigh-ins in, workouts out
  • Asking Ares sends that screen and a summary of your data through our server, for that answer only
  • Anonymous usage counts (which screens and features get used, never what you log) help decide what to build next

The privacy policy lists every piece of data and who can see it. You can delete your account in the app or here.
