Delete your account

Two ways. The first is immediate and needs the app; the second works from any device with email and takes up to 7 days. Either way, the logs on your phone are yours and stay there until you remove them.

In the app

  1. Open Settings from the header of any tab.
  2. Tap your name at the top of Settings (the account card).
  3. Tap Delete account, then confirm with Delete my account.

The account is deleted at once and the app signs out. If you want a copy first, tap Download my social data on the same screen; it shares one JSON file of every row stored about your account.

By email, without the app

If you no longer have the app installed or cannot sign in, send an email from the address your account uses:

  • To: showupfitnessapp@gmail.com
  • Subject line, exactly: Delete my ShowUp account
  • In the body: your username.

We may reply to that address to confirm the request came from you; we never ask for your password. The account is deleted within 7 days of the request and we confirm by email when it is done. We cannot delete an account from a different email address than the one it uses.

What is removed

Everything stored about your account on our server, at the same moment:

  • Your sign-in (email and hashed password), username and display name.
  • Your weekly cards, daily target checks and shared workout summaries.
  • Kudos, reactions, comments and messages you sent, including to people who still have the app.
  • Your challenges, buddy pairs, events, RSVPs and attendance marks.
  • Friendships, friend requests, invite links and codes, blocks, and reports you filed.
  • Your push notification token, if you had turned notifications on.
  • Moderation-queue entries for messages or comments of yours that were held or rejected.

Friends stop seeing you immediately. Reports other people filed about your account keep their date and reason for our records, with nothing that identifies you once the profile is gone. Database backups kept by our hosting provider age out within [FOUNDERS: the backup retention of your Supabase plan, for example 7 days]. Deletion cannot be undone; a new account starts from nothing.

What stays on your phone

Your workouts, food log, bodyweight, settings, personal records and everything Ares worked out are stored on your phone, not with the account, so deleting the account leaves them in place. To remove them as well, open Settings, then Data and backup, then Delete all data; or uninstall the app. Export them first as CSV or JSON from the app if you want a copy.

See the privacy policy for what an account stores and for how long.
